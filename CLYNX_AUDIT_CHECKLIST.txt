C. DEAL LOGIC AND ALERTS (ntfy)
=====================================================================
C1. Profit rule [REGRESSION]
  [ ] Every alert must reach your min margin (default 20% after eBay fees, you keep 84%) with a negotiation
      cut no larger than maxNegotiationPercent (default 15).
  [ ] "Under the Search For max price" alone must never make a deal (garbage alerts source).
  [ ] A $260 Series S worth $304 (needs 21% cut) must NOT alert.
C2. Pre-alert market check
  [ ] Clynx-found deals that would lose money on fresh numbers are held back. Watches are ALWAYS sent
      (by design). No sales data = no verdict (check what happens then).
  [ ] Skips re-check if exact product checked < 3 days ago.
C3. Alert text
  [ ] Whole stars only (1-5 emoji); no half-star glyphs.
  [ ] Says "Clynx" or "I", never "the AI". Every new user-facing string.
  [ ] Broken items: "Broken ones typically sell for $A-$B after eBay fees".
  [ ] Correct topic: main "clynxai"; Sam's Watch -> "clynxaisam". An alert on the wrong topic is a leak.
C4. Hidden gems
  [ ] Extras valued only if included in the price. "sold separately", "$15 each", "also have X for $120"
      must add NO value (extrasPricedSeparately).
C5. Bundles
  [ ] Lot value is never a straight sum (PS5 Pro $1100 + 2 controllers + HDD is ~$1166, not $1270).
  [ ] Hard-to-sell items (monitor/TV/printer, run time >= 14 days) left out of lot value.
  [ ] Breakdown quality and per-item lookups were only stub-tested, never confirmed live.
C6. Condition
  [ ] Broken/for-parts listings without includeBroken: old x0.25/x0.6 scaling; not taken seriously.
