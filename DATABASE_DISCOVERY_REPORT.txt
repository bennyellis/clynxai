Database discovery: reliability and accuracy

Status: fixed and tested. Follow-up (Beta 7 prep): the price-shape skip was removed completely, and persistence, budgets, queue and failure recovery got their own tests (see the end). Nothing is published.

What was wrong (root causes)

  * 1
      Cause: A photo look was only given to listings the text-only AI had already liked. With "Save OpenAI credits" on (the default) a vague title ("Gaming console works great") can't be valued from text, so it never got a look, was never identified, and never reached the Database.
      Evidence: 0 of the 27 listings stored in your Database have a photo analysis. Benchmark: 7 of 16 profitable listings found.
  * 2
      Cause: Photo results were thrown away. (a) `Store.saveScored` was `INSERT OR REPLACE` over a subset of columns, so every later re-save of a listing (green flag, mismatch, final verdict) silently set `photo_analysis`, its timestamp and the flaws back to NULL. (b) Even with "Save OpenAI credits" off, what the photos identified was never used to value a listing the text hadn't flagged.
      Evidence: (a) New test failed on the old code (3 failures), passes now. (b) Benchmark with the setting off: 14 of 16 profitable listings were looked at, only 7 reached you.
  * 3
      Cause: A listing whose price doesn't end in 0 or 5 was skipped unopened.
      Evidence: 3 of your 5 unopened skips were Xbox Series X/S at $71, $107 and $107.
  * 4
      Cause: A failed text read counted as "not a deal". An unreadable AI answer was saved as a rejection, never retried.
      Evidence: "Xbox one series x $400: Failed to parse AI response" in your Database.
  * 5
      Cause: No status. Skipped, unlooked-at, undecidable and genuinely unprofitable listings all read "skip: …".
      Evidence: Code: one log line for all of them.
  * 6
      Cause: A title-only deal whose photos showed something lesser kept its deal flag (the mismatch check only applied to a tracked item).
      Evidence: Code path; benchmark case "PS5 console" with only a controller pictured.
  * 7
      Cause: Nothing checked what the AI filed products under.
      Evidence: 30 of your 581 products (5%) have view / bundle / accessory wording: "iPhone (front view)", "Power Adapter", "Tempered glass screen protector", whole bundle sentences.
  * 8
      Cause: Waits between products. One photo worker, strictly first-come: a slow job holds everything behind it. The Database's background fill waited 5 minutes after every batch of 10 (you have 574 entries without a thumbnail), and a pair waited for the slower of the two. Related-product research ran strictly one at a time. The Live panel showed nothing while a product's Marketplace search was being set up, so it looked asleep.
      Evidence: Benchmark (setting off): average wait 103 s, longest 172 s.

What I changed

- research.ts (new, pure, tested): decides who earns a photo look; cleans and refuses product names; wording of every status.
- Speculative looks. A listing the text couldn't settle (vague title, unsure or unavailable AI read) now gets a photo look, inside an allowance: 3 per location visit, 30 per pass, never under $35. Deals, near matches, Watch candidates and hidden gems are unchanged and always looked at.
- Photo-first finds. If the photos name a specific product, it is valued from the Database's own numbers (free); if the Database has none, a market check runs (allowance: 2 per location, 12 per pass). The Deal Quality Gate validates it before anything is pushed.
- Text read retried once when the answer is unreadable; a failure is marked "no evidence", not "not a deal".
- Four honest states saved with every listing (new columns research_status/code/reason): *not researched*, *not enough evidence*, *rejected*, *confirmed unprofitable* (plus *researched*). Shown on the Feed card, counted in Activity ("Discovery summary for the last location: …"), and listed in the metrics.
- Price rule removed (follow-up). A price not ending in 0/5 no longer skips anything. It only puts a speculative photo look behind the others in the queue, and only for a title that names nothing tracked or known. The photo check (matchesClaim) is what catches the controller-titled-as-a-console case the rule was added for.
- Mismatch applied to title-only deals too.
- Product names cleaned before filing ("(front view)" and bundle asides removed; accessories, bare brands and sentences refused). Real names such as "Mini refrigerator (top-freezer style)" and "Xbox Series S Fortnite Bundle" are kept (checked against your 581 names).
- Saved photo analysis now survives re-saves.
- Throughput: two photo workers instead of one, served deal → Watch → speculative; the queue wait is now measured (research_queue_wait). Database fill waits 30 s after a clean batch (5 minutes only after a problem) with workers that take the next entry as soon as they're free. Related products researched two at a time. The Live panel now says "Searching Marketplace for …".
- Failed research job: the listing is marked "not enough evidence (RESEARCH_FAILED)" instead of waiting for ever.

Remaining limitations

- The AI is scripted in the benchmark, so real model accuracy is not measured; it shows the pipeline no longer discards what the model can identify. Blurry or dark photos stay "not enough evidence".
- 14 of your 27 stored listings were opened and then skipped as too old: the general browse has no Facebook "Date listed" filter (only the dedicated search does). I did not add one; this app avoids guessing Marketplace URL parameters and it needs a live check.
- When the allowance runs out a vague listing is "not researched (LOOK_BUDGET)": visible and adjustable, not lost.
- The 30 oddly named products already in your Database are untouched (needs your approval; Database > Review can propose them).
- With the price skip gone, more detail pages are opened (about 4 in 5 listings have a price not ending in 0/5), so scans spend more time per location; the per-location cap on evaluated listings still applies.
- The Watch active-check route and the eBay engine were not changed.

Settings that can explain what you saw

- Save OpenAI credits (default on): the main one (cause 1).
- Wait between passes (default 2-5 min): the "Cycle done. Sleeping in ~N min" line; applies after a whole pass over all locations, not after each product.
- Max listing age 24 h, General deal confidence "medium", Rotate search terms (5), listings per location 20.
- The shared AI service's daily limit (then every look says "AI unavailable", not rejected) and WatchCount's daily lookup cap.
- New, in config.json only: discovery.speculativeLooksPerLocation / speculativeLooksPerPass / marketLooksPerLocation / marketLooksPerPass (0 turns that part off).
