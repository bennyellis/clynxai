CLYNX BETA 9: DISCOVERY INVESTIGATION (Activity log of 10:54 PM - 11:04 PM)
============================================================================
Evidence is the log lines quoted below plus the code that wrote them. Nothing was published and no existing record or setting was modified.

ROOT CAUSES
 1. General discovery switched off by adding "xbox".
    Log: 11:02:46 "Turned general (unverified) deal detection off ...", then 11:02:48 "Chat added to Search For: xbox".
    Cause: two handlers in index.ts deliberately set generalDeal.enabled = false on EVERY add: search:addProducts (the Dashboard box, which wrote the 11:02:46
    line) and the add_search_item chat tool. Both were written for an earlier "focus on what I asked for" request and contradict "coexist unless I disable
    one". With general off, evaluateListing skips everything not on the Search For list as NOT_FOCUSED ("not one of your focused Search For items").
    Second defect: runCycle read the setting ONCE when the cycle started, evaluateListing read it live. After the switch-off mid-cycle every remaining location
    still browsed a full feed of 18 and then skipped nearly all of it instantly (Salem, Austin, Raleigh, ...): "found 18, evaluated 0".
 2. 18 found, 0-1 evaluated; the rest unaccounted for.
    Cause: only part of the outcomes were ever shown. Listings already seen on an earlier pass (store.hasSeenCached) and listings with no price left
    evaluateListing silently; listings the relevance filter excluded and listings cut off by the per-location limit were never mentioned. "Evaluated" counts
    only listings that were opened. The Discovery summary line counted only saved dispositions, so Austin showed 10 of 18, Montpelier 16 of 18 (after the
    9 evaluated were all "too old"), Augusta 0 of 18 and no skip lines at all (consistent with all 18 being duplicates; the log alone cannot prove it).
    Also: pages that were not the requested place (cause 4) re-showed the same few Richmond-area listings under a new location name, which is exactly what
    the silent "already seen" exit swallows ("$550, Woodbridge, VA" appears in the Montpelier and Salem passes).
 3. Research arriving minutes after the location was done; possible loss.
    Log: 10:55:52 "Analyzing 6 photo(s)" for Xbox series S ... 10:57:28 "Cycle done" ... 10:58:30 "Couldn't reach WatchCount/eBay in time for Xbox series S" and
    "Worth a look"; 11:01:33 a summary line "1 not enough evidence (AI_UNVERIFIED)" printed under a different location.
    Cause A (by design): the photo + price research for a listing runs in a background queue of two workers; a market check may take up to 150 s, so results
    land after the scanner moved on. Cause B (misattribution): the research's disposition was counted into whichever location was current when it finished,
    so late results were reported under the wrong place. Cause C (research LOST): when a market check timed out, runMarketCheck deleted the Database entry it
    had created for it while the check kept running "in the background to file its answer"; the late answer was filed into a deleted product and discarded.
    Queue contents are not lost on a location change (the engine and its queue persist); only an app close loses queued jobs, which the next start already
    marks INTERRUPTED.
 4. Wrong cities accepted (Augusta, ME read as Augusta, Georgia).
    Log: "Chose a new location: Augusta, ME" then "Confirmed now browsing Augusta, Georgia"; "Switched to Salem, OR, but the page still reads Richmond, Virginia ...
    searching there anyway"; "Search radius had drifted to 20 - changed it back to 100 miles".
    Cause 1: the confirmation compared the CITY NAME only (arrivedLabel.includes("augusta")), so Georgia passed. Cause 2: on a real mismatch the scanner searched
    the wrong place anyway. Cause 3 (why those places were used at all): location:setMode "country" restored the old guessed city-slug list marked
    locationResolved: true ("nothing to resolve - a static list"), undoing the earlier fix that made nationwide mode drive Facebook's own Change-location
    dialog. The slugs are not confirmed places (/marketplace/augusta opens Georgia; salem, montpelier stay on the account's default city). It also set the
    radius to 100 instead of the nationwide 500. Cause 4: the "Restarting the scanner to apply your location settings" path stopped and started the engine
    without ever resolving the new location, so a changed location was applied to the OLD browse list.
 5. Early exits that could hide legitimate discovery. Checked: too old (7 of 18 in places) and OFFICIAL_SHIPPING (8 of 18 at the first location) are intentional
    filters and were behaving as designed; the price-range floor ("under xbox's $150 floor") is the range the user set; "eBay search is on, nothing to search
    for" is correct. Previously-seen dedupe is correct but was invisible (fixed by the accounting). Research budgets did not stop anything in this log.
    The "worth about $348" on the iPhone 15 Pro Max ($580 market estimate x 0.6) is the existing minor-condition value factor from the photo analysis, not a
    matching error; left as is.
 6. Database creation -> research -> deal evaluation. The chain works (new items were added with photos, market check ran, the deal gate decided). The broken
    link was cause 3C: a timed-out check dropped its own product and result.

CHANGES
 - index.ts: adding a Search For item (chat or Dashboard box) no longer touches general detection; only the explicit tool does, and its prompt/definition now say
   adding a product is not a request to turn it off. search:addProducts and add_search_item otherwise unchanged.
 - scrapeEngine.ts: general-browse decision reads the live setting at each location; Facebook's location label is checked by city AND state (locationCheck.ts)
   and a definite mismatch skips that location (counted as not opened) instead of searching it; every pass logs a one-line account of all listings found
   (passAccount.ts): opened, skipped before opening, already seen, no price, failed to open, not relevant, left for the next pass / interrupted; the "Done at"
   line says how many listings are still in the research queue; a research result that finishes after its location was left is logged as a late result naming the
   listing, the location it came from and how long ago, and is not counted into the current location.
 - index.ts: location:setMode "country" leaves the location UNRESOLVED (radius 500) so the next start confirms each city through Facebook's dialog; the restart
   after a location change now resolves the location first, like a manual Start.
 - marketCheckSettle.ts + runMarketCheck: a timed-out check keeps its entry and files the late answer (prices: filled in; no prices or failure: removed, as an
   on-time check would). The returned result keeps the product id.
 Preserved: scam/shipping/age/price filters, product identity safeguards, Marketplace pacing (politeDelay unchanged; skipping a wrong location reduces visits),
 existing records and the user's saved settings (the account that had general detection turned off still has it off until the user turns it back on in Config
 or tells Clynx).

REMAINING LIMITATIONS
 - Not run against live Facebook. Whether the nationwide lookup confirms all 48 cities, and how long it takes at start, is untested here (state mode already works this way).
 - A location whose label cannot be compared (no state in the expected label, e.g. an opaque slug) still falls back to the city-name check and "search anyway".
 - Background research still finishes minutes after its location; it is now visible and correctly attributed, not faster. Queued jobs are still lost if the app is closed.
 - If every location of a cycle is skipped as the wrong place, the cycle counts as failed and backs off (existing behavior for unopenable locations).
 - A running scanner needs the new installer. This is not published or built; Beta 9 is still a source draft.
