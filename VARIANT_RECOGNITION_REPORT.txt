CLYNX BETA 8: UNIVERSAL PRODUCT VARIANT RECOGNITION
===================================================
Trigger: the transcript where Clynx told the user "Xbox Series X 2TB Galaxy Black Special Edition" was probably seller-invented, even after being given the
product's full name and a retailer link. Nothing here is specific to Xbox or Galaxy Black.

ROOT CAUSES (in the code, in the order they hurt)
 1. One fixed search. Research ran a single query "<name> specs variants common problems" (webResearch.ts) and the chat's web_research ran whatever one
    query the model typed. An exact-title search that returns nothing ended the investigation, and nothing told the model that means nothing.
 2. No evidence model. There was no difference between "nothing found", "only sellers use the name" and "a source says it is not official". The model
    filled the gap from its own memory ("Microsoft did not ship a 2TB...", "almost certainly seller-invented").
 3. Links were never read. No tool accepted a URL; the model said it "opened the Walmart URL" with nothing behind the claim.
 4. Variants were folded into the parent. research_product sent any request that fuzzy-matched an existing entry (isSimilarProductName let theme and
    edition words through) to that entry: its profile was rewritten about the variant, and its standard-model sold prices ($523) were shown for it.
 5. Comps ignored editions. compMatchesSpecs only compared digits, capacities, tier words and colors, so a standard console's sale could pass for a
    special edition's when the AI judge was off or wrong.
 6. No memory of findings. Each turn re-researched from scratch and could contradict the last one; nothing was stored.
 7. "Saved" claims. research_product fires the research in the background and returned before anything was written; nothing told the model that.
    A fresh stub was also created for any name, confirmed or not.

CHANGES
 variantIdentity.ts (new, pure)   Reads a name for capacity, color, edition words (including a themed word before "edition"), region, bundle and
     accessory; finds the parent a name is a more specific version of (never an accessory for a device, never a different tier such as Pro/Max, never a
     contradicted color or capacity); builds the search plan (exact title, "official", parent + distinctive words, manufacturer site search, the line's
     editions page, alternate spellings of capacities); editionConflict() for sold comps. No product or edition is listed anywhere.
 variantEvidence.ts (new)         Classifies sources (manufacturer / retailer / reference / marketplace / other), matches every identifying word of a
     claim against what a source actually says (single-letter model words only count next to their word, so a Series S page does not verify a Series X),
     and returns one of four verdicts: VERIFIED (manufacturer, or two independent non-marketplace sources), SUPPORTED (one retailer or reference source),
     UNVERIFIED (nothing, only seller listings, or only part of the name), DISPROVEN (an authoritative source next to the product's own words says it is
     not official / never made, and no authoritative source lists it). An empty search can only ever be UNVERIFIED. reconcileVerdict() keeps earlier
     findings: a thinner later look never downgrades them, a lone denial never flips a verified product.
 variantResearch.ts (new)         decideResearchTarget(): refresh the same product / create a confirmed variant linked to its parent / create an
     ordinary product / record nothing for an unconfirmed claim. makeClaimVerifier(): reuses what was found earlier in the session or saved in the
     Database, re-looks only when time has passed or the user brings a link. publicHttpUrls(): links Clynx may read (http/https only, nothing pointing at
     this computer or a private network).
 webResearch.ts / products.ts     Product research on a name that claims an edition runs the evidence check and puts the manufacturer's pages first in the
     writeup's sources. lookUpOnWeb reads a user's link first, goes beyond the exact title for specific names, and reports VERIFIED / SUPPORTED /
     UNVERIFIED / DISPROVEN in words the model must use; an unreadable page is reported as unreadable.
 chat.ts / clynxPersona.ts        web_research and research_product take a url. Standing rules for every chat: not recognizing a product is not
     evidence; unverified is not fake; never claim to have opened a page without its text; parents and variants have separate values; stay consistent with
     saved evidence; only say "saved" when a tool result says so. Summary prompts get the evidence status and may not call an unverified product fake.
 index.ts                          research_product now runs decideResearchTarget. A confirmed variant becomes its own entry with parentProductId set and
     its evidence saved; an unconfirmed one is NOT added (the answer starts "NOT SAVED") and the claim is kept on the parent. Messages say research is
     running and is saved when it finishes. A variant's comps and asking prices require the words that separate it from its parent.
 store.ts / types.ts              Local-only columns variant_status, variant_evidence, variant_claims (added the same way as the existing local-only columns,
     existing rows untouched). isSimilarProductName no longer folds a special/limited/themed edition into the standard entry.
 watchcount.ts / ebayActive.ts    Sold comps and asking prices drop a different edition's listings (editionConflict). No matching sales = no value.
 Database.tsx                     A product page shows its evidence status and the claims checked but not added.
 Beta 8                           package.json 0.3.0-beta.8, release-notes/0.3.0-beta.8.txt, BETA8_DRAFT.txt.

 New: variantEvidence.test.ts (71), variantResearch.test.ts (54), variantComps.test.ts (45), all offline with a fake search/page reader.
 Cases: Xbox Series X 2TB Galaxy Black Special Edition (exact title empty, found through the manufacturer-site search; verified from a linked page alone;
 the same page not verifying a different edition); legitimate variants (iPhone 15 Pro color/capacity, Switch OLED Zelda Edition, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Dyson
 V8, PS5 Slim Spider-Man bundle); seller-invented name (only marketplace listings: unverified, not filed); nonexistent name (empty search: unverified,

 "verified"); an unreadable link (admitted, not "fake"); links to localhost / private networks / file: refused; reuse of earlier findings, link triggers a
 new look, thinner look never downgrades; comps (standard sales never price a special edition, no sales = no value, the standard product is not priced

REMAINING LIMITATIONS
 - Not run against the live web. The search is DuckDuckGo's HTML page and many product pages (xbox.com, walmart.com) render with JavaScript or block bots,
   so the page text Clynx can read may lack the name; the evidence then comes from search snippets or stays unverified. First real use should be watched.
 - Source classes rest on lists (retailers, reference sites, marketplaces, and a small table mapping product lines such as iPhone/Xbox/Galaxy to their
   maker's site). A manufacturer not in the table is recognized when its site name is a word of the product name or the caller passes a brand hint; an
   unknown one is "other", which can support but never verify on its own.
 - "Disproven" depends on wording patterns near the product's own words. It is deliberately hard to reach.
 - A themed word with no edition marker ("Galaxy" alone) is only treated as distinguishing when a parent entry exists to compare with.
 - research_product still starts research in the background; the answer is honest that it is not saved yet, but it does not wait for the write.
   A research failure still stores the existing "(Research failed...)" text on the entry, as before.
 - A variant check can take several searches (up to 7 plus page reads) before the answer, roughly 10 to 40 seconds.
 - Scanner-created products from listing titles are not gated (existing records and the scanner's behavior are preserved); an edition-worded title now
   gets an evidence check during its research and its status is shown, but the entry is still created.
 - isSimilarProductName is stricter: a listing titled "... Special Edition" no longer fuzzy-matches the standard entry (intended), so a few loosely
   worded listings that used to attach to the standard product will not.
 - No Windows installer was built or signed, nothing was published, and no production data was touched.
