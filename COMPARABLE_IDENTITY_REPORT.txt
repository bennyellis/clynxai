CLYNX BETA 9: COMPARABLE-SALE IDENTITY VALIDATION
=================================================
Nothing was published. Existing records and historical valuations were not touched (a stored value changes only when that product is researched again).

WHAT THE CODE DID BEFORE (inspected)
 - Sold (WatchCount) and asking (eBay) results were filtered by compFilter.compMatchesSpecs against the SEARCH QUERY (plus a capacity from the product name),
   then by an AI "same product?" judge that was also shown only the query. In AI mode the query is AI-derived from the Marketplace title and can drop the
   model number, so "16" was not required by either gate. Model numbers were required only as "this digit appears somewhere in the title", so "iPhone 14
   case compatible with iPhone 16" passed, and nothing compared the number NEXT TO the word (iPhone 14 vs 16).
 - Accessory / lot / part rules existed only in the no-AI text check (titleLooksLikeProduct); with AI on, the judge alone decided.
 - Comparing a listing with the Database entry it was matched to (for its value) had no identity check unless the Phase 4 safeguard was switched on (off by default).
 - How many results were retrieved / accepted / rejected, and why, was never logged or kept.

WHAT WAS BUILT (extends the existing safeguards; no second matcher)
 compIdentity.ts (new, pure, reuses compFilter hard facts, colors.ts, variantIdentity.ts editions): classifyComparable() gives every candidate title one of
   exact / compatible / uncertain / rejected, with reason codes. Rejected: another model number (the number next to a word, glued or spaced: iphone 14 vs 16,
   v8 vs v10, rtx 3060 vs 3070, m1 vs m2), another generation (2nd vs 5th), another tier (Pro/Max/Plus/Slim/OLED/Ti...), another lettered line (Series S/X, One X),
   another capacity, another color, another edition (special/limited/themed), an accessory or "for/compatible with" listing, a part or not-working copy, a lot /
   multi-pack / bundle. Uncertain: model number or capacity not stated when the product has one, several models named. Compatible: the title is narrower than a
   generic entry, or an attribute that barely moves the price (color) is unstated. The product's NAME, the search QUERY and the Marketplace listing's own capacity
   and color all have to hold, so a loose query can no longer let a contradicting title through.
 Used for: watchcount.analyzeEbayComps (sold), ebayActive.fetchActiveAsks and its broken-variant path (asking), myStore reference, and the AI judge is now told
   the product's name too. Only exact and compatible results feed a value, a profit, a sell-through figure or a Database price; with none, the value is empty
   (insufficient evidence), never another model's.
 Listing -> Database entry (valuation, signals for the Deal Quality Gate, Feed price display, by-title lookups): a listing whose title contradicts the entry's
   identity (model, generation, tier, capacity, edition, "for" an accessory) gets no value from that entry. Missing information and a plain bundle listing do not.
 Logging: every research logs and stores "Sold results: retrieved N, accepted A (e exact, c compatible), uncertain U, rejected R (model number x6, tier x2, ...)"
   and the same for asking results, on Activity and the Discovery feed, plus a warning when nothing could be verified. The counts are kept on the product
   (local-only column comps_validation) and shown on its Database page.
 Automatic discovery, the pre-alert market check, Watches and the Deal Quality Gate all price through the same research and signals functions, so they inherit it.

TESTS (npm test: 62 files, 1881 checks pass, 1 existing failure unrelated; typecheck and build clean)
 compIdentity.test.ts (93): phones (iPhone 14/15/16, Pro vs Pro Max, capacity, Galaxy S23/S24, Pixel), consoles (Series S/X, One X, PS5 Pro/Slim, Switch OLED/Lite, Steam
 Deck OLED), computers (MacBook M1/M2, XPS 13/15, RTX 3060/3060 Ti/3070, iPad generations), appliances (Dyson V8/V10, Instant Pot sizes), collectibles (Funko 01/02,
 1st vs 2nd edition), accessories (cases, controllers, AirPods generations and replacement cases), bundles and lots, special editions, missing-vs-stated attributes,
 loose-query and listing-attribute cases; a 16-result mixed iPhone search priced only from the 5 verified sales with counts and reasons; only-wrong-model results
 give no value; a real Database refuses to value an iPhone 14 listing from an iPhone 16 entry. The existing identityGate test caught one regression during the work
 (a generic "Xbox Wireless Controller" entry) and drove the "narrower than a generic entry is compatible" rule.

LIMITATIONS
 - Not run against live WatchCount or eBay data, so where the iPhone 14 results actually enter (the AI query, WatchCount, or eBay) is not proven; both are now covered.
 - Rules read the words of a title in English. A title that states nothing (no model, no capacity) is uncertain and unused, which lowers sample sizes for products
   priced by capacity; that is the intended trade (fewer, verified comps; no value rather than a guessed one).
 - Products with no digits or tier words rely on the existing word checks and the AI judge; a "uncertain" listing is never promoted by the judge.
 - Re-researching a product that now finds no verified comps replaces its old value with "not enough evidence" (same as any re-research); nothing is rewritten in bulk.
 - The Deal Quality Gate itself was not changed: it reads validated sample counts and values; it does not show the comparable counts.
 - Needs a new installer; not built or published.
