Clynx Beta 30 - the Database gets smarter about what a product is
Read this first: almost all of this beta was written and tested with fake data and by code review. None of the new screens has been looked at on a real display, and none of the new background work has been run on a real Library or real Facebook pages. Please run the duplicate clean-up's preview before pressing Merge, and tell us what you see.
What changed
* One simple name per product. Names are decided by facts about each product line (Xbox Series X, Series S, the Switch and 3DS families, phones, headphones, mixers and more): "Xbox Series X Digital Edition", "... Robot White" and "... All-Digital" are one entry. Other names are kept as aliases, so old names, official names and typed names still find it. A bare line name ("Xbox Series X") keeps that name instead of guessing a colour. Colours that do not move the price are left out of the name, and the product page says so; names Clynx learns from eBay's item specifics (colours, sizes, editions real listings use) are shown on the product page and can be hidden with "not real".
* New entries are checked against the same rules everywhere an entry is made by Clynx or by you, so the same product under another spelling returns the existing entry. Entries pulled from the cloud are left alone and caught by the clean-up below.
* Duplicate clean-up (Database > Needs review > Same product under different names): "Show me what would merge" is a read-only preview ("N entries would become M"). Merge does exactly the previewed groups, after a backup, keeps the oldest entry, moves photos, listings, Watches, add-ons and notes onto it, keeps the other names as aliases, and can be undone for 30 days. Only names that mean the same known model, in the same category, are merged. Names that merely share words stay in Needs review.
* Mixed listings and galleries: a game found in a bundle listing ("Xbox Series X with Titanfall 2") gets its own entry in the platform's Games folder, linked to the console. A console gallery that already holds photos titled as a game is offered as a split in Needs review (backup first, one undo).
* Not sure which model? When a listing does not say which model it is (a phone with no lock state, a bare "Xbox Series X") and the possible models are priced differently, Clynx reads the description again; if it is still unsure it is shown as "Worth a look" with the reason instead of a deal. iCloud-locked, MDM, blacklisted and financed phones are For Parts and never alert.
* "Your price": for entries that matter and have no sold prices, Clynx adds a "Needs your price knowledge" item (a few a day at most, never the same entry within about 90 days). Your number is used at its low end until real sold prices arrive; when real sales differ a lot from your number a short message says so and the change is logged. A button opens eBay's sold listings in your own browser, and you can paste or load your own sold prices (Needs review > Import sold prices); they go through the same identity checks as everything else.
* When a sold-price search returns nothing, the activity feed now says why (a security check, WatchCount's daily limit, or a real "no sales").
* Add-ons: the premium an add-on adds is measured from your stored and imported sales (with it minus without, never more than it sells for alone or half the base) and labelled MEASURED; with too few sales it is labelled a guess. Standard controllers of any colour are one shared entry. "Add a game..." and "Add an accessory..." open a searchable list.
* Pulsing dots on the Database icon, the Needs review button, folders and entries mark things that wait for you; a dot goes away only when you open that item (or it is resolved). "Mark all as seen" is in Needs review.
* Smaller: a settings file saved with a byte-order mark no longer counts as damaged; Escape closes the top dialog; product pages open at the top; Send stays clear of the floating assistant button; library search finds any words and other names; merge suggestions say how identical-looking entries differ; every new entry records why it was made.
What is NOT fixed or not proven
* Nothing here has been seen on a real screen or run on your real Library. The duplicate clean-up, the gallery split, the price questions, the dots and the toast are the least proven.
* The identity step only reads the title and description. Reading photos, labels and eBay image search for an unsure listing is not built, so such listings are held back as "Worth a look" rather than resolved.
* Add-ons are measured on the product page only; the scanner's deal scoring still uses its fixed weights. Per-entry sold statistics (30/90-day counts, sell-through) and the green frame on a family's price are not built. The Wow message compares with the identity check only, not condition and inclusions.
* Facts are learned only from eBay's item specifics (never seen working on a live request) and only change names for colours.
* Still open: the misspelling case that answers "Short answer: yes" without price data, a product with many accepted asks that still shows "no price yet", a poster that gets a chat answer but no card, photo source badges, and the scan decision is not yet split into separate stage functions.
* Clear all and Reset with cloud delete your server's copies too when you are signed in. Installing this update changes nothing in your library by itself; the clean-up, splits and price questions only act when you approve them.
