Clynx Beta 17 - Edition research in chat, Reset Clynx
* Fixed: asking chat to research special editions or variants ("research all special editions", "look up the SNES variant") now really searches the web. It reads catalogue, specialist and maker pages, lists every named edition with where it was found and how well supported it is (confirmed by the maker or a specialist/two sites, or reported by one source), and remembers them. If nothing is found it says why: the search engines were blocked (naming each), or no page it read mentions that edition. Asking is no longer rate-limited. Marketplace category pages (eBay) are not used as proof.
* New: Config > Account > Reset Clynx. It returns Clynx to a freshly installed state on this computer while keeping you signed in. It erases your whole Library (products, photos, folders, Trash), everything Clynx learned (research, corrections, the blacklist), your Feed history, Watches, Search For list, custom instructions and every Config setting, and the welcome tour runs again. It keeps your sign-in, your saved keys and your Facebook logins (tick the box to remove those too).
* It always makes a safety backup first and does nothing if the backup fails. Your Database is saved in Config > Account > Database backups (use Restore to go back), and a copy of your Database and Config is also kept in a reset-backups folder next to it.
* If you use Clynx Cloud, a second box lets the reset also remove your synced products, Watches and corrections from the cloud. Other computers on the account lose them too. Leave it off and the next sync brings them back.
* You must type RESET to confirm. Clynx restarts by itself when it is done.
* Unchanged: everything in Beta 16.
