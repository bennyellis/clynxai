Clynx Beta 13 - safer Library, smarter photos, condition-aware prices, calmer chats
* Fixed: a photo of an Xbox Series X could be filed under Series S (and other models that share a name). Photos now go through ONE gate that checks the title and, where there is no title, the picture. Disc drive vs All-Digital, graphics-board design, region, carrier lock and model code are now part of a product's identity, so a gallery can no longer mix them. Anything unclear is set aside for your review instead of filed.
* Fixed: game cases and accessories are no longer filed as the console they mention (an Xbox game or a Switch dock cover does not enter the console's gallery), while genuine bundle titles still pass.
* Fixed: Clynx no longer names a Library entry from what a photo looks like, and the photo audit no longer moves or deletes photos on its own. It marks suspects "Needs review" and you decide. Marked photos stay visible, dimmed.
* Fixed: deleting a Library entry that has photos failed on some databases. Deleting now moves it to a Trash (kept 30 days) and you can restore it.
* New: Library folders you create yourself ("My folders"): New folder, rename, move, restore, and move products in. They are local to this computer. The automatic categories are still there.
* New: Needs review and Trash panels, an Organize card on each product, per-photo actions (keep, set aside, move, replace, make primary), and Undo for recent Library changes. Nothing is merged, deleted or renamed automatically.
* New: ask Clynx in any chat to organize the Library ("put my Switch games in a folder"). It asks you to confirm before changing more than one thing, and everything it does can be undone.
* New: reference photos are picked for trust and variety, not because they are the newest. A photo you chose is protected from the photo cap. Collage images are no longer stored as thumbnails.
* New (shadow mode): condition is read from both the listing text and its photos as specific defects (cracks, dents, missing parts and so on) with an estimated repair cost or value share, shown as a range. A listing whose photos show damage the seller did not mention is flagged. In Beta 13 this is recorded and shown on the listing card but does not yet change prices or alerts; switch it on with config.json "beta13": {"conditionEvidence": "enforce"} after you have checked it.
* New: all four chats (Assistant bar, Database, Activity, Discovery) now accept up to four photos, show what Clynx is really doing with a live timer, and have a Stop button. Photos are shrunk before sending so they fit the shared Clynx Cloud limit.
* New: when Clynx needs a decision it shows buttons instead of asking you to type A or B. You can still type.
* Changed: chat replies are 1-3 sentences unless you ask for detail. Prices, margins and scam warnings are never shortened away.
* Safety: a backup is made before the first database change. The changes are additive, so downgrading works, but entries you moved to the Trash stay hidden from Beta 12. Every new behaviour has a switch in config.json "beta13" (off / shadow / enforce).
* Unchanged: your data, sign-in, Watches, automatic updates, the 24-hour freshness window, the seller checks, the scam-bot and placeholder-price rules, Search For persistence, and the separate Discovery and Activity chats.
