Clynx Beta 12 - finds deals it used to miss
* Fixed: Clynx read the whole Facebook page as the listing's description, including other sellers' cards ("Related searches", "Today's picks"). Another seller's "PlayStation 5 as is for parts" could cut an Xbox's value by 75%. Clynx now reads only the listing's own text. On a real database this affected nearly one in four opened listings.
* Fixed: damage wording is now read in context. "No damage", "not broken", "no dead pixels", "screen not cracked" and "no power issues" no longer count as damage; real damage ("cracked", "for parts, does not power on") still does.
* Fixed: having several Search For items no longer quietly limits general deal discovery to your Search For list while general discovery is on. Your Search For items are looked at first, then everything else.
* New: a listing Clynx already saw is looked at again, a limited number of times, when its asking price drops or when its earlier research could not finish. It updates the same listing and never alerts twice.
* New: a price check that finishes late now re-judges the listings that were waiting for it, and sends the alert if one now qualifies. Listings kept as "worth a look" are re-checked at the start of every pass with whatever the Database has learned.
* New: when the AI alone said a specific product is "not a deal", Clynx now checks what it really sells for (within the same small allowance of market lookups) instead of treating its guess as a verdict. Without real numbers the listing stays marked as a provisional estimate.
* New: a listing held back by your seller checks still isn't alerted, but its reason now says when it would otherwise have cleared your margin on the Database's numbers.
* New: the Dashboard shows "Listings fully evaluated" with an honest breakdown (saved, opened, skipped before opening, ruled out by a rule, uncertain, deferred, failed, pending) instead of counting every saved row as evaluated.
* Reference photos: a photo of a video game, an accessory, or a different model is no longer filed under a console or phone that merely shares a word with it (this was how a Guardians of the Galaxy game ended up on an Xbox Series X Galaxy Black Edition). Images that come with a title are checked from the title with no AI call; images with no title need the picture check and are not saved if it fails or says no. The same rules now protect comparable-sale prices and the photos listings add to Database entries. Existing photos are not touched; scripts\photo-audit.cjs lists suspects for you to review.
* New: Search for variants (Dashboard > Add product, and a switch on each Search For entry; off by default). On, "iPhone 15" also accepts 15 Plus / Pro / Pro Max among the listings Facebook already returns. It makes no extra searches, never accepts a different model, a case or part, or a game, and prices each variant from its own sales, never the base model's.
* Discovery chat: messages now read top to bottom (oldest first) with the box at the bottom, separate from a live "Automation" panel for scanner activity. Side by side on wide windows, tabs on narrow ones.
* Activity: switch between All, Activity Feed and Chat with Clynx, filter events, and use "Ask Clynx" on an event to start a question about it.
* Database and Library remember where you were (product, folder, search, scroll) across sections and restarts, and clicking the active sidebar icon again takes you home.
* New: your Search For list is now remembered. The products you are searching for (with their price ranges, include-broken and Search for variants settings) are still there the next time you open Clynx. Restoring them does not start scanning: it still starts only when you press Start. Add, change and remove all save immediately; Recent searches work as before. Custom instructions and the general-deal switch still start fresh each launch.
* Unchanged: your data, sign-in, Watches, Database, automatic updates, the 24-hour freshness window, the seller checks, and the scam-bot and placeholder-price rules.
