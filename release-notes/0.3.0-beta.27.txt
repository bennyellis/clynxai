Clynx Beta 27 - fewer guessed products, failures that don't overwrite descriptions, deletes that stick
What changed
* One typed phrase no longer fans out into a pile of guessed products. Names the AI suggests from memory are only candidates: they must be a more specific version of what you typed (a Canon R6 Mark II no longer produces R5/R8/R3), they must be confirmed by web sources, and Clynx examines at most 3 per phrase, spaced out, two at a time. It stops if the AI is paused. Unconfirmed names are kept as notes on the base product, never filed.
* A failed research no longer replaces a product's description. The product keeps its description, category and place and is marked "needs retry" with one plain sentence. Clynx retries by itself after 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours (only while the AI is reachable), then leaves it alone. The eBay price-check failure shown in chat is one sentence instead of a wall of browser flags.
* A verified edition takes its official name and is linked under its base product, even when the plain base is created later (for example the Galaxy edition under the Xbox Series X).
* A request typed with a product is no longer part of its name: "xbox series x. find all variants and add-ons" researches the Xbox Series X. "research the ..." / "find me ..." commands and keyboard mashing are no longer part of a product's name or filed as products. Some questions ("is a used series s worth flipping?") are caught, but the check is rough in both directions: about 10 of 25 plain questions TESTER tried ("what is the best drone", "are airpods pro waterproof") still become products, and titles that contain words like you / I / much or a "?" ("Can You Feel It vinyl", "Is This It? vinyl", "Should I Stay or Should I Go single") are still refused as questions. Titles that merely start with a question word ("Can Am Maverick X3", "Is This It vinyl") now research normally. An entry named exactly what you typed is the one researched, even when a game's title contains it (an entry named with an extra word, like "Xbox Series X Console", can still trigger the game-or-console question). Spelling repair is stricter and no longer turns real words into other words ("holo" is not "Halo"). A plain name like "kitchenaid artisan mixer" refreshes the existing entry instead of duplicating it.
* "Clear all" now queues the matching deletes for your server, which it never did (that is why cleared products came back after a sync). Queued deletes for products the server no longer holds are forgotten after a complete sync check that returns at least one product (an empty answer is not trusted, so if your server holds nothing for your account, old queued deletes just stay queued; nothing is sent). The Clear all dialog now says so when cloud sync is on. "Reset with cloud" also queues deletes for the products your server lists, not just the ones on this PC. If you use Reset with cloud, it will remove your server's copies too.
* Add-ons, first version: a new Add-ons section on the product page. Add-ons seen in listings are suggested and start switched OFF; you confirm them, give each a price, or dismiss them (dismissed ones never return). The estimate is labelled ROUGH: it is a range, capped at half the product's value, built from fixed weights, with no sold listings behind it. It never changes the product's stored price.
* The build stamp no longer says "dirty" just because unrelated notes are lying around the project folder.
What is NOT fixed or not proven
* None of the above has been run against the live app and OpenAI; it was tested with fakes and throwaway databases, plus a code-only review. The first real use after installing is the proof, especially the Galaxy parent link, the automatic retries, and the cloud deletes against a real server.
* OpenAI web search through your own server has still not been seen working end to end.
* Prices may still be missing: sold-price lookups (WatchCount) often return nothing, and nothing reports why.
* The chat can still say "still saving" after a failed run, photo source labels can be wrong, and Library search only looks in the folder you're in.
* Typed names can still create a duplicate in some cases ("dewalt dcd771" next to "DeWalt DCD771C2 20V Drill", "samsng galaxy s24 ultra" next to "Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra 256GB"). A new generation (Canon EOS R6 Mark II) can still be filed under the older model (R6). A short attribute after a full stop ("iPhone 15 Pro Max. 256GB. Blue") is treated as a request, so the size and colour are not part of the researched name. A repaired spelling can leave mixed capital letters in the stored name. After the four automatic retries a product stays marked "needs retry" until you press Research.
* A listing sold "with controller" is still treated as an accessory; the bare title "Xbox Series X Galaxy" is still accepted as Galaxy with low certainty; "Xbox 2TB Galaxy Black Series X Console" can still be taken by the plain console. The "I read ... as ..." line depends on the chat model including it.
* No identity card, no combined photo+words check, no Halo game/console split, and Add-ons are first-version only (no profit rule or alert uses them).
* Nothing in your library is changed by this update. Clynx does not flush the 109 old queued deletes; they are dropped only after a complete sync check shows the server doesn't hold them.
