Clynx Beta 26 - the words you type are what Clynx researches
What changed
* What you type is what gets researched. "xbox series x galaxy edition" is no longer swapped for an older entry that merely looked similar (such as an invented "Xbox Series X Galaxy/White"). A spelling slip or a swapped word order ("Xbox X series", "xbox seris x galxy edition") is repaired and Clynx tells you what it read. If a phrase could be a game or a console already in your Database ("halo infinite"), Clynx asks which you mean; a phrase that is not a product name is not filed.
* Research replies no longer stall with "What should I focus on in the writeup?". Clynx says what it started, then offers follow-ups.
* Product look-ups, the edition check and chat look-ups now try OpenAI web search first, with a budget of their own (20 searches an hour by default; the setting is openaiLookupPerHour): a repeated question is answered from memory for a week, searches are spaced out, and only two run at once. OpenAI web search itself is on by default (it was off, and no screen could turn it on).
* The Galaxy edition stays its own product under the Xbox Series X. Photos and sold prices that belong to a plain console, another Xbox (360, One), a game (Guardians of the Galaxy, Starfield, Halo and look-alike titles), a controller, or a broken / for-parts / box-only listing are no longer filed or priced under it, and the plain Xbox Series X no longer takes Galaxy edition items. "Limited Edition" and "Special Edition" now count as the same thing, and a title that names the Galaxy edition but leaves out the storage size is no longer held back (consoles only).
* Listings that cannot say whether they are a console or a game ("Halo Xbox Series X Brand New", "PS5 Spider-Man Brand New", "Zelda N64") are held as uncertain until a photo can confirm them: no photo is filed from them without a picture check and they are not used for a price. Clynx needs both the photos and the words.
What is NOT fixed or not proven
* OpenAI web search has never been seen working through your own server. Search rows in the server's usage table were empty when last checked; the first real search after installing this beta is the proof.
* Prices may still be missing: the sold-price lookups (WatchCount) often return nothing, and nothing in the app reports why.
* A failed research run can still save an error message as a product description (D2). Photos are not yet cross-checked against listing words in one step; titles held as uncertain do not get promoted back into prices by their photos yet.
* Edition spelling variants are only partly handled in sold prices; a listing sold "with controller" is still treated as an accessory and loses its sold price; a Galaxy title with no word "Edition" (for example "Xbox 2TB Galaxy Black Series X Console") can still be taken by the plain Xbox Series X. The bare title "Xbox Series X Galaxy" is accepted as a Galaxy listing with low certainty.
* No identity card, add-ons, or Halo game/console split yet. Nothing in your library is changed by this update.
