Clynx Beta 15 - Library folders you control, deeper product research
* New: rename and delete the automatic Library folders, and give any folder a picture. Open a folder tile's "..." menu (or the menu in a folder's header): Rename, "Make a picture with Clynx", Upload a picture, Choose from this folder's photos, "Use automatic pictures again", Delete. Names and pictures you set are kept: Clynx's research never undoes them, and new products that arrive in the folder go into the renamed folder.
* Deleting a folder never erases products. You choose: move its contents to the parent folder, or send the folder and its products to the Trash (it asks first). Both can be undone, and the Trash keeps things 30 days.
* Folder pictures are labelled by where they came from (generated artwork, picked from the folder's own products, your upload, your choice). Generated artwork is decorative only and can never become a photo of a real product. A console folder never takes a game's picture. Automatic generation is limited (3 a day, once per folder, off in Save-credits mode); a picture you chose is never replaced.
* The chat does the same things: "rename Nintendo Consoles to Nintendo Hardware", "make a better thumbnail for Nintendo Consoles", "use this product's photo as the folder picture", "undo the last folder change". It reports what actually happened.
* New (shadow mode): when Clynx is unsure what an item is, it works it out from the photos with no title in mind, asks only the questions that tell similar models apart (for example one HOME button or three on a 3DS XL), and looks up special editions from what it sees. It records what it WOULD conclude in Activity > AI Activity and changes no listing, price or alert yet. It never calls a factory edition verified from a photo.
* New: check a model name. Ask the chat "is the RTX 4080 Ti a real model?" and it reads the maker's own lineup. It only says a name is not in the lineup when it really read one, and offers real models from that page; otherwise it says it could not check.
* New: Clynx keeps what it researches (makers, models, editions, what they look like, sources) as separate knowledge. Researching an edition never adds a product to your Library.
* Damage: photos are judged against rules for glare and reflections, and against the product's normal shape when Clynx knows it. A photo that does not show the back of a phone says so instead of assuming it is fine. A defect in both the text and the photos is counted once. Photos show appearance only, never whether something works.
* Optional, off by default: OpenAI web search for hard research. Turn it on with config.json "beta13": {"openaiSearch": true}. With Clynx Cloud the server owner must also set AI_SEARCH_ENABLED=true (see the server notes). It is not Google. It is used first only for hard cases, at most 12 searches an hour, never in Save-credits mode, and falls back to the free engines.
* For testers: scripts\reference-fetch.cjs and scripts\visual-eval.cjs download licensed reference photos and run a real-photo accuracy check using the same AI route as the app.
* Unchanged: your data, sign-in, Watches, automatic updates, the seller, scam and placeholder-price rules, and everything in Beta 14 and earlier.
