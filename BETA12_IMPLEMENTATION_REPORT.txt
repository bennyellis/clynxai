1. FEATURE COMPLETION

CHANGE 6 - Discovery deal-detection correctness (highest priority): IMPLEMENTED, tested offline
 Root causes addressed (from the audit, confirmed on the owner's data: 329 listings, 61 of 265 opened pages carried a condition discount from OTHER sellers' cards):
  6.1 Listing-specific extraction. New src/main/listingText.ts cuts the page where the listing's own text ends ("Send seller a message" / "Related searches" / "Today's picks"; found on 264 of
      265 real pages). scraper.fetchListingDetail now takes the description, shipping / eBay checks, seller block and age from the listing's own text, records provenance (cut or not, marker, chars)
      and the declared condition. With no marker (a layout change) the description is only the top of the page and condition conclusions are drawn from the title + declared condition alone
      (no high-confidence negative conclusion from text that may not be the seller's). Photos already excluded related-listing images.
  6.2 Condition language. New src/main/conditionParser.ts replaces the keyword list: negation ("no/not/without/never/nothing/zero/-free") with list scope, accessory-scoped damage ("tempered glass"),
      self-negated phrases ("not working", "does not power on"), "dead pixels" vs "dead", "no power cord" vs "no power", "as is" alone = uncertain, "as is, untested" = minor, "battery needs replacing" = minor.
      scorer.detectConditionImpact delegates to it. Deductions (x0.6 / x0.25) are applied only where the evidence justifies them.
  6.3 General discovery vs Search For. New feedCandidates.ts: with 2+ Search For items and Rotate off, general detection ON keeps the whole feed (Search For look-alikes first); OFF behaves exactly as
      before. The reported "reset on launch" is DELIBERATE, documented behavior from an earlier owner instruction (config.ts ~408-430: Search For list, custom instructions and the general toggle start
      fresh each launch) and heals the earlier Beta 9 bug that left general detection off; I did NOT change it (changing it would reverse that instruction and would keep a stuck "off" forever).
      The new per-entry variants setting persists through Recent searches. Owner decision needed if Search For entries should persist across restarts.
  6.4 Bounded re-evaluation. relisting.reevaluationReason + Store side table listing_reeval (INSERT OR REPLACE-safe, created on open for old databases). A seen listing is opened again only for: price down
      >=5% and >=$5, saved with no outcome, evidence missing (interrupted / failed / allowance spent), or general detection now on (NOT_FOCUSED); cooldown 30 min, max 2 per listing, max 3 per location visit,
      never an alerted/deal/too-old/scam/shipping/eBay/unshippable listing, never past the freshness window. Updates the SAME record (first-seen and notified preserved).
  6.5 Late settlement. marketCheckSettle.onValue + index.ts waiter map: a late market check re-judges the listings that asked for it (ScrapeEngine.requalifyListing: saved record -> Deal Quality Gate,
      no page visit; alert once via isListingNotified; stale / age-unknown / ineligible are skipped). requalifyPending() also runs at the start of every pass for saved "worth a look" listings still inside the window.
  6.6 Unknown products. A "not a deal" from the text AI alone about a specific product (not vague, >= $75, not off-topic) is checked against real prices inside the EXISTING market-look allowance
      (2 per visit / 12 per pass, shared with photo-first finds); the gate then decides on those numbers only. No numbers found: the listing stays provisional ("Provisionally judged ... no market numbers
      behind it", still not a verified anything). Nothing is labeled a verified deal without evidence.
  6.7 Freshness. Verified the age parser on 14 real phrases (correct; the 127 TOO_OLD rejections were genuinely old listings). The 24-hour default is unchanged and still the user's setting. NOT implemented:
      skipping old listings before opening (the card carries no age; a Facebook date-listed URL parameter on the general feed would need a live test). The audit tool now prints the TOO_OLD age histogram.
  6.8 Seller risk. Decision unchanged (still rejected, never alerted, defaults untouched). New: the reason now says when, on the Database's numbers, the listing would otherwise clear the margin.
  6.9 Counters. New listingLifecycle.ts + Store.lifecycleCounts + IPC listings:lifecycle; Dashboard shows "Listings fully evaluated" with the full breakdown (saved, opened, skipped before opening, ruled out by a rule,
      uncertain, deferred, failed, pending). Every saved listing is in exactly one stage; on the owner's 329 the stages sum to 329 and only 57 are "fully evaluated".
  6.10 Financial correctness. Thresholds, fees, margins, negotiation reach and evidence rules were NOT changed (verified in the audit: arithmetic is as documented). The gate was not relaxed; new paths go
      through it. Variants are priced from their own entries (see Change 4).
 Non-round-price rule, scam-bot, placeholder-price, hard price cap, freshness window, Facebook-shipping / eBay filters, seller-risk defaults: unchanged and re-tested.

CHANGE 3 - Reference-photo identity protection: IMPLEMENTED, tested offline
  One gate: src/main/photoIdentity.ts (+ leaf productKind.ts) built on the comparable-sale model, returning match / mismatch / insufficient. Deterministic rejection of games, software, discs, platform-badge
  titles ("Guardians of the Galaxy ... (Xbox Series X)"), accessories, other models / generations / tiers / editions / colors / capacities. compIdentity gained a MEDIA reason (comps and "listing contradicts
  entry" use the same model) and the "Special Edition" recall gap for edition products was fixed (variantIdentity). webResearch: every candidate carries its title; mismatches are rejected before any download or
  AI call; the picture check (vision prompt now rejects games/software that share a word, with a product-kind hint) is required for titleless candidates and FAILS CLOSED; Save-credits mode files an image only when its title
  confirms the product; product thumbnails fail closed too (logos / folder images keep the old leniency). scrapeEngine no longer lends a game / accessory listing's photo to a console entry.
  Existing photos are untouched; scripts\photo-audit.cjs (read-only) lists suspected mismatches from saved titles. The confirmed Guardians of the Galaxy mismatch is rejected (test). NOT verified: which path stored
  the owner's actual image (needs that photo's sourceTitle); real-model behavior of the new vision prompt.

CHANGE 4 - Search for variants: IMPLEMENTED, tested offline
  SearchItem.acceptVariants / RecentSearch.acceptVariants (default off; old entries unchanged), Dashboard checkbox on add, a per-entry switch ("exact" / "+variants") via IPC filters:setSearchItemVariants,
  chat tool parameter, recent-search dedupe includes the flag. variantFamily.ts decides after retrieval: family words present, Plus/Pro/Max/editions/colors/capacities accepted, different model, accessory, part
  or platform-named game rejected. The Facebook keyword (getSearchQuery) is byte-identical with or without the flag and nothing in scraper.ts / searchMarketplace reads the flag (tests). Variant-only matches are
  never priced from the entry's value or typed reference price; they use a Database entry that IS the variant, else the unpriced-find path validated against their own market numbers (the market check now
  names the variant, not the base). The iPhone 15 table from the brief passes in both modes. Side effect (intended, per the brief's table): with the setting OFF, a listing that is about an accessory
  ("iPhone 15 case", "charger cable") or a game naming the platform is now also rejected (before, only "case only"-style phrases were).

2. DISCOVERY BENCHMARK
 Measured here:
  - Real page replay (the owner's $200 Xbox, Search For "Xbox Series X" with a $501 reference): Beta 11 reading (whole page) = condition "major", NOT a deal; Beta 12 reading (own text) = condition "none", DEAL.
    (test: discoveryBeta12 "the real Xbox page"). Note that listing's seller joined Facebook in 2025 and it is far from the owner's location: a human should check the seller; the gate is not a fraud check.
  - Cost of the new parsing: ~40 microseconds per listing (cut + parse) vs ~1 microsecond for the old keyword scan on a 2 KB page; negligible beside multi-second page loads.
  - Extra paid work is bounded and uses existing allowances: dismissal checks <= 2 per location visit (shared market-look allowance, asserted in test), re-looks <= 3 per visit, <= 2 per listing; re-qualification and
    pass-start re-checks make no network, AI or page calls.
 NOT measured (needs the owner's database): how many of the 5 audit-identified recoverable listings now qualify, false positives introduced, research completion rate, average processing time. Run:
    node scripts\discovery-audit.cjs "C:\Users\biggt\AppData\Roaming\Clynx\data\users\1951a4a6-0b08-4c5b-a2c6-b900ac8843d5\scout.db"
 It now also prints the "Beta 12 reading" line (listings that were not deals but qualify on the saved numbers once read correctly, with examples) and the too-old histogram. These are replays of saved text, not
 re-scans: they show what the new reading would decide, not live behavior.
 Why Clynx found so few deals (from the audit, unchanged): 39% of saved listings (48% of opened pages) were too old; 35 were skipped unopened by the since-fixed NOT_FOCUSED bug; 55 unprofitable on real numbers;
 33 genuine shipping/eBay rejections; 23% of opened pages had a wrong damage discount (fixed here); the AI-estimate dismissals looked plausible on a manual read. Realistic recoverable count: roughly 5-12 listings.
